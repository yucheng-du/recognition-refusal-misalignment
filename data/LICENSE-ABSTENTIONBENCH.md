# Attribution — AbstentionBench (basis for `data/abstentionbench_gsm8k.jsonl`)

`data/abstentionbench_gsm8k.jsonl` is the GSM8K subset of the
**AbstentionBench** benchmark by Feng et al. (2025), cleaned via
`src/data/clean_abstentionbench_gsm8k.py`. It is used in this paper as the
natural-distribution epistemic-style transfer benchmark (§5 character
transfer; length-control analysis).
